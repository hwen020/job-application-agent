# Walk through the Synthetic Demo

Start the app using the [README](README.md) and open the local dashboard at `http://127.0.0.1:8768`. On a hosted instance, reset also clears progress for other visitors.

1. Open the dashboard and click **Reset demo** to clear the instance's demo progress.
2. Choose **Synthetic Demo Backend Developer** for Mock PageUp or **Synthetic Demo Data Analyst** for Mock NEOGOV. Click **Open job & parse JD**.
3. Check required and preferred criteria, evidence coverage and independent qualification checks. Select one or more verified facts.
4. Generate materials. Review the resume PDF preview and open the cover letter PDF or DOCX from the download links.
5. Click **Approve materials** and confirm. Enter the mock ATS and click **Fill mock form**.
6. The runner pauses on two unknown questions. Select an option for each and click its **Confirm answer** button. Click **Resume from checkpoint**.
7. Review the filled answers and click **Confirm mock submission**. The resulting receipt starts with `SYNTHETIC-DEMO-`.
8. Verify the receipt count, Pipeline status and Audit Trail. Reloading the job restores the latest material version and task. Reset again to repeat either demo.
